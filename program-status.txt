Breakdown of programs and status:


### DISCOVERY EVIDENCE GATE

The first-stage gate is implemented. It aggregates standardized calls at the
original-contig level, retains every contig with at least one qualified viral
call, preserves cellular/plasmid conflicts as ambiguous candidates, and emits
candidate/noncandidate FASTA files plus complete audit and summary tables. It
does not reconcile taxonomy. The candidate FASTA is the intended input to the
future CheckV quality/boundary stage.

Resource scheduling now separates the total local executor budget
(`max_cpus`/`max_memory`) from per-tool CPU, memory, and time requests. Utility
tasks use one CPU; multithreaded discovery tools receive their supported CLI
thread option from `task.cpus`, while Deep6 and DeepMicroClass2 are restricted
through their native thread-pool environment variables. Shared-server,
dedicated-server, and viCAT-database-build profiles are available.
